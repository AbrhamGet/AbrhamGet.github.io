12M+ views in a year

Habtambet

Social media management, content production, campaign execution, influencer outreach

146,000+ followers gained in a year across platforms, built on consistent short-form video and creator partnerships.

Habtambet's Telegram channel had 70,000 subscribers who weren't doing much beyond scrolling past. I built a Friday competition series to give people a reason to open the channel, and it grew into a community of 15,000 people showing up weekly.

On TikTok and Meta, the "Habtam" mascot came out of wanting the brand to have a face people would recognize mid-scroll instead of another betting logo. Green persona, top hat, sacks of cash — it's not subtle, but it's memorable, and recall on this kind of content depends on being unmistakable within a second or two.

4M+ content views, 4 months

One Mobile

Content creation and production direction

20,000+ followers gained in four months, driven by scroll-stopping product and lifestyle content.

One Mobile needed content that did two things at once: look good enough to stop the scroll, and say enough about the phone to move someone toward buying it. Every post split into a lifestyle open and a clear product close, which is a big part of why the account picked up over 10,000 followers in some months without ad spend behind it.

110K+ TikTok followers gained

Orbit Electronics

Social media management, content, production, campaign planning

100,000 net followers across platforms in a year, backed by a New Year's campaign built to convert reach into follows.

Refrigerators aren't inherently exciting content, so the approach was a dual-funnel: one lane of lifestyle storytelling to get attention, one lane of detailed product reviews to answer the questions people actually have before buying an appliance. Running both at once let the account grow reach and still convert people who were closer to a purchase decision.

3M+ views in 2 months

Techno Z Engineering

Content creation and production direction

43,000+ followers gained in two months for a manufacturing brand.

This is an industrial baking equipment brand, which is a genuinely hard sell for social media. The content leaned into showing the machines actually working — injera coming off the plate, dough proofing — the kind of process footage people don't expect to find satisfying, but do. The views followed.

1.6M+ views in 2 months

SAF Home Decor

Social media management, content, production direction, campaign planning

6,000+ followers gained in two months through product-led, everyday-use content.

Home decor content works best when it looks like something a customer already wants in their own space, not a showroom photo. I kept the content close to real, everyday use — someone unrolling a rug, styling a shelf — instead of catalog-style shots.

TM Coffee

Google Ads, SEO content, and social strategy for a Canadian B2B wholesale coffee buyer, alongside support for US-facing wholesale distribution

Built and troubleshot Search campaigns targeting Canadian wholesale buyers and wrote page-level SEO content for the brand site — my first sustained work marketing into North American B2B markets.

TM Coffee sells wholesale to Canadian buyers, which is a different job than posting for a local audience. The Google Ads campaigns had to be built around how B2B buyers actually search — by product spec and origin, not by a brand name nobody in Canada knows yet — and the SEO content had to slot into the site without competing against TM Coffee's own product pages for the same keywords.
