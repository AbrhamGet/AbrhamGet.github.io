01 About

I'm a digital marketer specializing in content creation, strategy, and execution. I've developed, managed, and delivered high-performing campaigns that translated directly into increased sales, stronger brand awareness, and a more consistent digital presence for the businesses I work with.

I currently freelance for five local companies while building my own startup agency, Quill Communications, offering complete digital marketing services — content strategy, social media management, paid advertising on Meta and Google, video production, and influencer campaigns — to brands spanning electronics, chocolate, telecom, engineering, home decor, hospitality, and wholesale coffee.

A few accounts take me outside Ethiopia entirely: I manage a Canadian B2B wholesale coffee account and support US-facing wholesale distribution, which means building Google Ads campaigns, SEO content, and creative that speaks to North American buyers, not just the local market.
